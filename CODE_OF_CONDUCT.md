# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the CubeTimer community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards, and may take other actions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing CubeTimer in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/danilolutz). Do not use public issues for sensitive conduct reports.

All reports will be reviewed and handled respectfully and confidentially when possible.
